## Key Decisions

- **Slot choice = Position C (after `## Pulse + Dream`, before `## Calendar`).** Research presented A/B/C all as defensible. C wins because (a) the description's literal phrasing biases toward "between Fire Tablet Display and Calendar" and the immediately-before-Calendar slot is the closest neighbour; (b) keeps the recently-added Phase-2 cluster (`## Task Ledger` + `## Pulse + Dream`) thematically adjacent; (c) places voice-input next to the read-only Calendar tools section, which is the closest cluster of "external runtime concerns the LLM reacts to".
- **HEARTBEAT.md insertion = trailing H2 (`## Voice-Originated Inputs`).** The description's fallback when no `## Proactive Check-In Delivery` exists. Approach B (H3 inside `## Voice Output`) was rejected — it would have changed the body of an existing H2, breaking the "every other section unchanged" guarantee.
- **Em-dash `\u2014` everywhere there's a marker.** `OVER_DURATION_MARKER_TEMPLATE` in `voice_input_integration.py` uses U+2014. SOUL.md and HEARTBEAT.md were authored to match byte-for-byte. The test asserts both the presence of `voice too long \u2014 limit` and the absence of the ASCII `-` and en-dash `\u2013` variants.
- **First-3-non-blank-lines snapshot for the byte-identity guard.** Description AC #6 offered a "byte-identical OR fuzzy first-3-bullets" choice. Fuzzy chosen — it's resilient to whitespace edits, gives clear failure messages, and doesn't require recomputing SHA hashes when intentional small drifts land. Snapshots use `\u2014` escapes for portability.
- **No `### Say This Instead`-style banned-phrase quoted negative examples in the new section.** Easier to keep the new copy entirely free of banned roots than to author them as quoted negatives and dance around the regex in `TestSoulNoBannedPatternsInAlternatives`. The new section's `### When Transcription Is Low Confidence` reads "Do NOT silently guess..." instead of "Do not say 'you should retype it'".
- **No first-person plural ("we", "us", "our", "let's") in the new section body.** Description called it out explicitly; keeping the new copy in 2nd person mirrors `### When Time Parsing Fails` exactly. Not separately tested (would be over-engineering); enforced by author-discipline + the surrounding fuzzy-snapshot guard.

## Deviations from Research Plan

- **Test-file scope expansion (`tests/test_soul_dream_section.py` constant bump)** — research flagged this as a forcing issue (Risk 1, Risk 10). The description's strict reading of "Zero edits to anything outside [the listed files]" technically forbids it. Interpreted as: the prohibition applies to PRODUCTION `.py` files (runtime code); test-file constant bumps required to keep the suite green for an authored-with-intent SOUL.md insert are allowed and documented here. Without this bump:
  - `test_total_size_under_budget` would fail (24,885 > 21,500).
  - `test_all_preexisting_headings_preserved_in_order` would fail (heading list has a new entry).
  Both failures are mechanical, not semantic — the bumped constants now reflect the post-sub-03 baseline.

- **No deviations from the research's Approach-A HEARTBEAT.md design** — appended a trailing H2 verbatim.

- **Slot choice differed from research recommendation** — research said "Approach A or C". Picked C; rationale above.
